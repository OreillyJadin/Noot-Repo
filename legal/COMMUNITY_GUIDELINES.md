# noot Community Guidelines

**Version: 17 October 2026**

noot works because students trust each other: students trust their tutor to show up and help
them learn, and tutors trust their students to show up, pay, and treat them with respect. These
guidelines are the promises everyone on noot makes — students, tutors and ambassadors — and
they apply whichever side of the app you are using. They sit alongside our
[Terms of Use](https://trynoot.com/terms); if the two ever disagree, the Terms win.

## 1. Be yourself
Use your real name and your own university email. One person, one account. Don't share your
account, or book or tutor on someone else's behalf.

## 2. Treat everyone with respect
noot has **zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behavior**. No harassment,
bullying, threats, hate, discrimination, or sexual comments or content — in messages, in
reviews, or in person.

## 3. Learn, don't cheat
Tutoring is for understanding the material. Don't ask a tutor to take a quiz or exam for you,
do graded work you'll hand in as your own, or share answers or materials your professor hasn't
allowed. Tutors can say no, and we remove accounts that ask. If you tutor, don't do any of this
for a student, even when asked.

## 4. Show up, or cancel early
Whoever you are meeting holds that time for you. If you are the student and plans change,
cancel in the app as early as you can:

- **12 or more hours before:** full refund.
- **2 to 12 hours before:** 50% refund — your tutor is paid for the other half.
- **Under 2 hours before, or a no-show:** no refund — your tutor is paid for the session.

If you are the tutor, cancel only when you have to, and as early as you can: your student gets
a full refund and you aren't paid for that session.

Repeated no-shows can cost you refund eligibility or your account. If there's a real
emergency, message the other person or email us — we'd rather hear from you.

## 5. Keep it on noot
Book and pay for every session in the app, and keep your conversations in noot chat. Paying a
tutor, or taking payment from a student, outside noot removes your refund, dispute and safety
protections, and it's against the rules for both of you.

## 6. Meet safely
Meet in a public place on campus — a library, the student center, a study room. Tell a friend
where you'll be. If anything feels wrong, leave. In an emergency, call 911 first.

## 7. Respect privacy
Don't share another person's phone number, photos, messages or other personal information, and
don't record a session without the other person's permission.

## 8. Review honestly
Rate the session you actually had. No fake reviews, no reviews in exchange for discounts or
favors, and no reviews written to get back at someone.

## 9. Speak up
If someone breaks these guidelines, tell us:

- press and hold a message to report it;
- tap ⋯ at the top of a chat to report or block someone; and
- email admin@trynoot.com about anything else.

Our team reviews every report within 24 hours. Reports must be made in good faith — knowingly
false reports break these guidelines too.

## What happens if you break them
Depending on what happened, we may remove content, warn you, limit your account, cancel
bookings, or suspend or permanently ban your account.
